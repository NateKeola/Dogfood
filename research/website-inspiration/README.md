# Funded-company website inspiration

A practical reference library of recently funded companies' websites: what each site does well, why it is worth studying, and the funding evidence that qualified it.

- [2026-10-27 data (JSON)](data/2026-10-27.json)
- 20 companies across software, AI, biotech, hardware, aviation, robotics, health, gifting, and creative tools.
- Funding announcements dated October 27, 2025–October 27, 2026.
- 3 sites reviewed visually and by text; 17 reviewed by text only. Each record states its review method. Text observations describe content, navigation, and messaging—not a verified visual assessment.

The working tracker remains in the owner's private workspace. Its URL and account identifiers are deliberately not published here. These dated snapshots contain public-source research only.

## Daily collection rules

Target 20 new, unique company domains per day, with the recurring collection scheduled for 8 a.m. America/Los_Angeles beginning October 28, 2026. The schedule is managed outside this repository; this README is not an executable job.

1. Use a rolling 12-month funding-announcement window relative to each collection date. Record the announcement date, round/amount as reported, and a source URL; do not substitute a founding date, valuation, or unannounced future raise.
2. Prefer the company's own announcement or an attributable company-issued release. Preserve qualifiers such as “more than,” mixed secondary transactions, and unspecified round types.
3. Check prior snapshots and the working tracker for duplicate canonical domains. Different paths or www/non-www versions of the same site do not qualify as new companies.
4. Visit the website and record one concrete, useful design or communication observation. Mark `Visual + text` only when actual rendered visuals were inspected; otherwise mark `Text`.
5. Seek a varied mix of industries. Do not fabricate sources, funding facts, or observations to fill a quota; document any shortfall in that day's collection.
6. Save a new dated snapshot; preserve prior records and user edits. If correcting a factual error, document the correction rather than silently rewriting history.
7. Learn from general patterns, information architecture, and messaging. Do not copy proprietary imagery, branding, or code.

## Data fields

`date_added`, `company`, `website`, `industry`, `funding_date`, `round_amount`, `funding_source`, `design_inspiration`, `review_method`.

Dates use ISO 8601 (`YYYY-MM-DD`). Funding amounts are quoted descriptions, not normalized financial calculations. Eligibility is assessed at collection time, not perpetually. Company statements and design notes are research inputs, not investment recommendations or independent verification of financial performance.
