## Rundown

The reel opens on the cover, briefly shows the illustrated plant spread, then spends most of its time on eight pages from the back of the book. The sequence is: how medicines were administered, how they were categorized, a lookup table, and the beginning of a preparation section. The table is a historical medicines reference, not an herbal-only list. The cover returns after about 01:12; no additional book pages are shown after that.

The source is a moving camera, not a scan: framing, motion, and the reel’s burned-in captions obscure parts of some pages. Page numbers are read from the headers where possible and checked against the consecutive page turns. The screenshots document what the reel shows, not whether the narrator’s claims are true. Do not use the listed doses or preparations for treatment.
