### Reusable workflow
1. Ingest authorized footage and transcribe it with timecodes.
2. Rank candidate clips by hook, standalone clarity, and payoff; choose the clip before spending time polishing it.
3. Render a 9:16 draft with readable captions, stable framing, and a reusable brand preset.
4. Check caption accuracy, audio, framing, pacing, and the exported file, then send it to a review queue. Keep publishing a separate step.
5. Establish a baseline on one real clip before trying to produce a batch. Compare time to an approved cut and how much manual cleanup it needed.

### What to verify before adoption
- The supplied PDF describes Groq or offline transcription; the current repository README names AssemblyAI as the primary path and lists additional connections. Verify dependencies, costs, and where footage goes before trying the actual kit.
- The guide describes YouTube scheduling, not verified Instagram publishing. Don’t assume this repo handles Instagram or that automated quality checks are enabled in every configuration.
- A small first test: use one approved video, render one branded vertical draft, review it on a phone, and leave account connections and publishing off. The useful pattern to borrow first is an editorial selection rubric plus a review gate, not the whole install.
