# Affirmation reference collection

A source-linked collection of currently gathered affirmations. This is public-source reference material, not a personal journal, behavioral tracker, or record of anyone's finances or beliefs.

Updated: 2026-11-13 (America/Los_Angeles).

## Coverage and use

- The six lines in Set A were supplied in the request context as a verified partial set.
- Sets B and C are original paraphrases of themes observed during direct reel playback. Some captions were missed, so these are **not complete transcripts or an exhaustive list of every spoken affirmation**.
- Similar ideas are kept grouped by source; exact duplicate lines are not repeated.
- These are reflective prompts, not promises that thoughts alone will produce financial or other outcomes.
- Keep future additions source-linked. Label exact supplied wording, paraphrases, and uncertain material clearly. Do not add private correspondence, email addresses, delivery schedules or personal tracking data.
