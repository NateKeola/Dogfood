## Survey context, kept separate

[Ahrefs' survey](https://ahrefs.com/blog/seo-pricing/) polled 439 SEO providers and manually checked their websites. Its detailed monthly-retainer section gives $501–$1,000 as the most common band, reported by 20.4%; its summary uses a different 23%/$500–$1,000 statement. Use the explicitly labelled detailed section and do not reconcile these silently. Its agency “average” of $3,209 assumes the upper end of each respondent's price band; that is not an invoice-weighted mean. The source has global geography and mixes agencies, consultancies and freelancers. The retrieved publication metadata is December 17, 2023; survey fieldwork date was not established. It is historical context, not a November 2026 market median.

No Reddit anecdotal quotes are included: community research is access-gated. Published competitor offers prove those offers exist, not that they are profitable, effective or widely purchased.

## Implications for an honest menu

Care, one-time fixes, content production, managed SEO and AI monitoring are different obligations. Avoid using expensive managed SEO quotes to justify a thin monitoring add-on. Show what changes, what stays recurring, the expected operator time and the cancellation/export terms. For AI visibility, report sampled queries and dates; don't sell a universal score or guaranteed inclusion. For a low-cost pilot, start with existing analytics and a bounded audit rather than committing to one paid tool subscription per client.
