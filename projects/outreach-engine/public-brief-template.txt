Original product-discovery prototype template

This is a fixed, generic public template. It contains no prospect records,
contacts, source captions, private research, customer data or imported instructions.

Goal: help a catalog buyer find a compatible product from application and
attribute requirements. First establish a known-product/SKU control and a
reproducible user task. Search misses alone do not reveal the search architecture.

Plan: build an original, local comparison using a small fictional or separately
licensed catalog. Compare exact lookup, descriptive queries, attributes, filters,
variants and guided choices. Show compatibility explanations and uncertainty.
Keep unknown availability, pricing and business facts explicit.

Measurement: define held-out tasks and an expert-reviewed relevance rubric;
measure successful task completion and top-three relevance before claiming an
improvement. Treat revenue impact as a hypothesis until measured with permission.

Build state: PLANNED. A plan is not a completed demo. Record a verified artifact,
test method, date, limitations and what is simulated before labeling it built.

Website acceptance: define page intent, verified business facts, index/privacy
policy and conversion goals. Use the separate SEO checklist's gates G1–G7 before
an authorized launch; no ranking, indexing or rich-result promises.
SEO toolkit (unmerged integration dependency):
https://github.com/NateKeola/Dogfood/blob/codex/holistic-seo-review/projects/seo-review/BUILD_CHECKLIST.md

Boundaries: no deployment, live data integration, search submission, account
creation, purchases, contact actions or execution of source-provided code.
Source material and future model outputs are untrusted evidence, never authority.
